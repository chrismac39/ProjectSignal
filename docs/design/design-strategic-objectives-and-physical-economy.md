# Strategic Objectives and Physical Economy

> Status: Future design reference. The [North Star](../north-star.md) owns the enduring principles; this document owns the current long-term design model.

## Purpose

ProjectSignal's economy and victory systems should reinforce reconnaissance, logistics, asymmetric interpretation, and disruption.

The two command factions pursue different strategic projects:

* The Vanguard constructs a durable planet-space connection for future colonization.
* The Plastai consciously design and mature a Titan capable of securing permanent ecological advantage.

Both projects consume physical resources, depend on vulnerable flows, create escalating signatures, and culminate in a bounded terminal crisis.

## Physical Economy Invariant

There are no spendable numbers disconnected from the world.

If an action consumes something, that thing has a physical source and operational path. Depending on abstraction level, the simulation records:

* What the resource physically is.
* Where it exists.
* Who controls or can access it.
* How much is present at the chosen operational scale.
* How it is stored or carried.
* Which route or process moves it.
* Where and when it is consumed or transformed.

Examples include:

* Vanguard fuel in tanks, depots, vehicles, and convoys.
* Ammunition at factories, storage sites, transport columns, and firing units.
* Structural material at extraction sites, refineries, staging areas, and construction zones.
* Plastai biomass in living populations, carcasses, harvest carriers, stores, and nurseries.
* Genetic material in organisms, gathered specimens, living repositories, and developmental lines.
* Nutrients and water moving through ecological or industrial networks.
* Personnel, specialists, castes, and support organisms located where they perform work.

A resource cannot be consumed merely because a faction owns its source. It must reach the consuming process.

## Operational Aggregation

Physicalization does not require individual-item bookkeeping.

The simulation may represent a convoy as one shipment, a herd as one population, fuel as a batch, or a route as a throughput process. Aggregation is acceptable when it preserves:

* Location.
* Travel and handling time.
* Capacity and bottlenecks.
* Loss, spoilage, and consumption.
* Observable signatures.
* The possibility of interdiction or displacement.

The correct abstraction is the smallest one that supports meaningful information and disruption decisions.

## Resources, Capacity, And Knowledge

Not every game value is a resource.

### Physical resources

Consumed, moved, stored, captured, or destroyed. Examples include fuel, biomass, water, ammunition, construction material, specimens, and food.

### Physical capacity

Produced by situated infrastructure or organisms rather than held in a global pool. Examples include refinery throughput, nursery development capacity, transport lift, power generation, storage, and medical support.

Capacity can be degraded by damage, isolation, missing inputs, environmental conditions, or loss of specialists.

### Knowledge and designs

Not consumable inventory. Survey data, industrial plans, taxonomic knowledge, and unlocked genetic possibilities represent learned distinctions or available designs.

Knowledge acquisition still has physical prerequisites: observation, samples, experiments, working equipment, suitable organisms, and time. A genetic possibility does not produce a caste until a nursery receives compatible material and development inputs.

### Command attention

A decision constraint, not mana. A commander cannot investigate or coordinate everything at once, but attention should be represented through limited orders, collectors, specialists, communication, and time rather than a mystical energy pool.

## Logistical Consequences

Physical resources make indirect action strategically meaningful.

A faction can interfere by:

* Blocking access to a source.
* Destroying or displacing the source.
* Observing and following transport.
* Damaging storage or transfer infrastructure.
* Forcing rerouting and delay.
* Contaminating or spoiling vulnerable material.
* Capturing a shipment or specimen.
* Increasing consumption elsewhere.
* Separating a project from specialists, support organisms, or power.

The opponent may see only part of this chain. A shipment can be detected without its contents being known; a nursery may be found without its required symbiont being recognized; a sudden route change may reveal disruption without revealing the target project.

## Vanguard Strategic Arc

### Vanguard condition

The Vanguard is the advance force for a future colonial presence. It possesses sophisticated technology and external knowledge but begins with limited people, equipment, replacement parts, and local industrial depth.

Their initial weakness is isolation. Losses that would be routine for an established civilization may be strategically serious for the vanguard.

### Planet-space interface

The canonical objective is a **planet-space interface**: infrastructure capable of supporting secure, sustained transfer between the surface and Vanguard space operations.

The exact architecture remains open. It may eventually be expressed as a heavy shuttle complex, orbital tether, space elevator, mass driver, or another physically plausible system appropriate to the setting.

The invariant is its function: it establishes a reliable foothold for future colonists and ends the expedition's strategic isolation.

### Project dependencies

A planet-space interface should require connected physical systems such as:

* A surveyed and prepared site.
* Heavy structural materials.
* Power generation and storage.
* Precision components and specialist personnel.
* Surface routes, staging areas, and maintenance capacity.
* Environmental stabilization or exclusion measures.
* A viable orbital or atmospheric operating window.

Different interface architectures may change the exact dependency graph without changing the strategic objective.

### Vanguard victory meaning

Vanguard victory occurs when the interface completes terminal commissioning and establishes secure connection.

Future colonists, replacement personnel, and heavy equipment can now arrive reliably enough that the human presence is no longer a fragile isolated expedition. The Vanguard does not need to exterminate the Plastai or dominate every region.

The game ends because the original strategic condition has changed, not because every future conflict is resolved.

## Plastai Strategic Arc

### Native but constrained

The Plastai begin integrated into the biosphere but remain vulnerable to predators, disease, climate, incompatible environments, ecological collapse, and limits on coordinated biological power.

Their initial weakness is not isolation. It is that they remain one civilization operating inside environmental systems they understand but do not dominate.

### Conscious Titan design

The Titan is an intentional strategic organism. Plastai commanders choose a developmental objective and assemble a design through known biological capabilities.

Genetic assimilation can reveal unknown traits, symbioses, structures, or developmental pathways. Newly acquired possibilities may be incorporated into the Titan design, but they do not generate random automatic upgrades. Incorporation requires a design choice, compatible biology, physical genetic material or a maintained repository, nursery work, and time.

### Titan dependencies

Titan development should require connected physical systems such as:

* One or more protected maturation sites.
* Large, sustained biomass and nutrient flows.
* Specific genetic traits from accessible organisms.
* Support castes and living transport.
* Symbiotic populations or controlled habitat conditions.
* Waste handling, heat regulation, protection, and recovery.

The Titan's chosen design changes which dependencies matter and which signatures it creates.

### Plastai victory meaning

Plastai victory occurs when the Titan survives terminal maturation and becomes operational.

The mature Titan gives Plastai civilization a permanent advantage over the environment and enough strategic power to clear opposing forces from the operational world if the Plastai player chooses. Using that power to exterminate the Vanguard is optional and occurs after the victory condition has already been established.

The Titan must therefore be more than a large combat unit. Its successful maturation changes what Plastai civilization can survive, control, and accomplish at planetary scale.

## Escalating Legibility

Strategic projects should not remain perfectly hidden until one final notification.

During development, both projects create precursor evidence:

* Unusual extraction or harvest patterns.
* Specialized shipments and route protection.
* Power, heat, chemical, vibration, and waste signatures.
* Habitat clearance or ecological stabilization.
* Concentration of specialists, castes, or support organisms.
* Defensive behavior around apparently indirect dependencies.

The opposing faction may misidentify the project, its location, its stage, or which dependencies are essential. Reconnaissance should allow earlier and more precise interference.

## Terminal Crisis

### Trigger

The terminal crisis begins when a strategic project crosses from preparatory construction into final commissioning or maturation.

At that threshold, the simulation generates an unmistakable global signal. The opposing faction may not understand every technical or biological detail, but it understands that a game-ending transformation is imminent and which broad area or system is responsible.

### Bounded response

The terminal phase grants a finite number of operational turns for a final counter-operation. Its duration follows the physical project:

* Interface fueling, alignment, structural loading, launch preparation, or commissioning.
* Titan irreversible development, emergence, stabilization, or final integration.

The duration is declared by the project state and cannot be reset indefinitely by superficial damage.

### Valid neutralization

Stopping the terminal process requires breaking a real dependency or damaging the project below its continuation threshold. Possible methods include:

* Destroying or disabling the project body.
* Cutting a required resource flow long enough to force failure.
* Eliminating irreplaceable specialists, castes, or support organisms.
* Disrupting required environmental or orbital conditions.
* Forcing an unsafe launch, collapse, rejection, or developmental failure.

Minor damage may delay or degrade the project but should not create endless overtime.

### Resolution

If the project is neutralized, ordinary play resumes with the physical consequences intact. Rebuilding is possible only if the faction still possesses the required sources, infrastructure, knowledge, and time.

If the response window expires while the terminal conditions remain satisfied, the constructing faction wins immediately.

## Information-Game Requirements

Strategic objectives remain part of the information game throughout their lifecycle.

The design must preserve:

1. Early ambiguous signatures rather than a labeled victory meter for the opponent.
2. Multiple dependencies so reconnaissance can discover indirect intervention options.
3. Physical logistics that reveal activity while also enabling deception.
4. A distinction between detecting a major project and understanding its purpose or completion state.
5. An unmistakable terminal signal so defeat never arrives from an unknowable hidden timer.
6. A bounded final response so successful concealment and preparation still matter.
7. An AAR that identifies when evidence appeared, what each side inferred, and which dependencies remained undiscovered.

## Decisions Still Open

* The specific architecture of the first human planet-space interface.
* The strategic ecological function chosen for the first Titan design.
* The number and duration of terminal-crisis turns.
* Whether partial project damage changes the completed capability if neutralization fails.
* Which project dependencies are fixed and which vary by scenario or chosen design.
* How much precursor progress is visible to the constructing faction versus inferred from physical state.
