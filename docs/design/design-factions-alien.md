# Alien Faction

> Status: Future design reference. Titans, resources, castes, expansion, and victory systems are outside Prototype 0.

## Purpose

The Alien Faction represents a native intelligent species embedded within the planet's ecology.

Unlike Humans, Aliens do not build an industrial civilization.

They project power through biology, ecological knowledge, adaptation, and the growth of the Titan.

The Alien experience should feel fundamentally different from traditional strategy game factions.

Aliens do not seek to conquer the world.

They seek to consume, reshape, and ultimately harvest it.

---

# Core Identity

## A Native Civilization

The Alien Faction is not wildlife.

The Alien Faction is not a collection of environmental hazards.

The Alien Faction is a civilization.

It possesses:

* Goals
* Planning
* Agency
* Infrastructure
* Logistics
* Expansion priorities

Alien players directly control units and make strategic decisions.

---

## Biological Rather Than Industrial

Humans solve problems through technology.

Aliens solve problems through biology.

Human civilization creates:

* Factories
* Roads
* Vehicles
* Sensors
* Refineries

Alien civilization creates:

* Castes
* Nutrient networks
* Adaptation structures
* Gene repositories
* Ecological influence systems

Both factions build infrastructure.

The infrastructure simply takes different forms.

---

## Part Of The Ecosystem

Aliens are native to the planet.

They are not external invaders.

They evolved within the world's ecological systems.

Aliens possess extensive understanding of:

* Species behavior
* Predator territories
* Migration patterns
* Nutrient flows
* Ecological relationships

However, they are not masters of nature.

The ecosystem remains an independent force.

---

# The Titan

## Central Organism

The Titan is the biological foundation of Alien civilization.

The Titan consumes:

* Biomass
* Nutrients
* Genetic material

The Titan produces:

* Castes
* Biological infrastructure
* Adaptations
* Expanded influence capacity

The Titan fulfills a role similar to the Human industrial base.

---

## Strategic Importance

The growth of the Titan represents long-term alien success.

Expansion is not pursued purely for territory.

Expansion is pursued to acquire:

* Biomass
* Nutrients
* Genetic diversity

The world itself is a resource.

---

# Resources

## Biomass

Represents usable biological material.

Consumed by:

* Titan growth
* Caste production
* Biological construction

---

## Nutrients

Represents the energy required to sustain alien systems.

May originate from:

* Ecosystem harvesting
* Biological processing
* Specialized structures

---

## Genetic Material

Represents biological diversity.

Obtained from native species.

Used to unlock new adaptations and future caste forms.

Genetic diversity is strategically valuable.

Not all regions are equally valuable.

---

# Castes

## Directly Controlled Units

Aliens possess directly controlled units known as Castes.

Castes provide:

* Reconnaissance
* Expansion
* Harvesting
* Combat
* Logistics

Castes are the primary means through which players interact with the world.

---

## Adaptation

Castes are biological products of the Titan.

New forms emerge through genetic acquisition and adaptation.

Expansion into biologically diverse regions creates strategic opportunities.

---

## Biosphere Integration

Alien Castes should appear as natural products of the world's ecology.

Their observable signatures should not make them trivially distinct from the planet's broader biosphere.

Humans should struggle to distinguish:

* Wildlife
* Alien Castes
* Alien biological infrastructure

without direct observation.

---

# Ecological Influence

## Core Concept

Aliens may influence any native species.

Influence is not magical.

Influence requires:

* Resources
* Infrastructure
* Biological mechanisms

Influence must have a physical presence within the simulation.

---

## Universal Potential

Any species may become:

* A threat
* A distraction
* A resource
* A sensor network
* A weapon

The limitation is cost.

Not eligibility.

---

## Scarcity

Aliens cannot influence every species simultaneously.

Players must prioritize opportunities.

The strategic question is not:

"Can this species be used?"

The strategic question is:

"Is this species worth using right now?"

---

# Information Model

## Ecological Awareness

Aliens possess deep understanding of biological systems.

Alien players naturally recognize:

* Species
* Predator behavior
* Migration patterns
* Ecological disruption

The ecosystem is readable.

---

## Industrial Blindness

Aliens do not naturally understand Human systems.

Human activity generates signals.

Examples:

* Heat
* Vibration
* Resource extraction
* Noise
* Construction

Aliens must interpret those signals.

Human intentions are not automatically known.

---

## Signals And Interpretation

A physical signature is a potentially detectable consequence in the world.

An observation is evidence an alien collector actually acquired from a signature.

A report describes that observation in alien terms. Meaning emerges through commander interpretation.

This principle applies to both factions.

---

# Mobility

## Ecological Mobility

Aliens project power through ecological pathways.

Examples include:

* Forest networks
* Migration routes
* Burrow systems
* River ecosystems
* Biological corridors

Alien mobility is tied to ecological connectivity rather than infrastructure.

---

## Species-Specific Risk

Not all native species are friendly to Aliens.

Some species:

* Ignore Aliens.
* Avoid Aliens.
* Threaten Aliens.

The ecosystem creates genuine movement constraints.

---

# Expansion

## Ecological Expansion

Aliens expand to acquire:

* Biomass
* Nutrients
* Genes
* Ecological leverage

Expansion is both economic and evolutionary.

---

## Ecosystem As Territory

Control does not necessarily mean occupation.

Alien influence may be projected through ecological relationships.

Territory can be contested indirectly through ecosystem manipulation.

---

# Relationship To Humans

## Humans As Ecological Disruption

Humans are viewed as a new and disruptive force.

They alter:

* Nutrient flows
* Habitats
* Migration routes
* Ecosystem stability

Human activity changes the planet.

---

## Humans As A Resource

Humans are not merely enemies.

They represent:

* Biomass
* Genetic opportunity
* Ecological disruption
* Strategic risk

The Alien relationship with Humanity is more complex than simple territorial conflict.

---

# Victory Philosophy

Aliens are not attempting to build a larger nation-state.

Aliens seek to transform the world.

Success is measured through:

* Titan growth
* Biological adaptation
* Ecological control
* Resource consumption
* Long-term planetary transformation

The Alien Faction wins by reshaping the future of the planet itself.
