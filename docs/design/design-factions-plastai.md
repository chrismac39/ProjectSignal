# Plastai

> Status: Future design reference. Titans, resources, castes, expansion, and victory systems are outside Prototype 0.

## Purpose

The Plastai are a native intelligent species and organized civilization embedded within the planet's ecology.

Unlike the Vanguard, the Plastai do not build an industrial civilization.

They project power through biology, ecological knowledge, adaptation, and the growth of the Titan.

The Plastai experience should feel fundamentally different from traditional strategy game factions.

The Plastai do not seek to conquer the world.

They seek to consume, reshape, and ultimately harvest it.

---

# Core Identity

## A Native Civilization

The Plastai are not wildlife.

The Plastai are not a collection of environmental hazards.

The Plastai are a civilization.

It possesses:

* Goals
* Planning
* Agency
* Infrastructure
* Logistics
* Expansion priorities

Plastai players directly control units and make strategic decisions.

---

## Biological Rather Than Industrial

The Vanguard solves problems through technology.

The Plastai solve problems through biology.

Vanguard civilization creates:

* Factories
* Roads
* Vehicles
* Sensors
* Refineries

Plastai civilization creates:

* Castes
* Nutrient networks
* Adaptation structures
* Gene repositories
* Ecological influence systems

Both factions build infrastructure.

The infrastructure simply takes different forms.

---

## Part Of The Ecosystem

The Plastai are native to the planet.

They are not external invaders.

They evolved within the world's ecological systems.

The Plastai possess extensive understanding of:

* Species behavior
* Predator territories
* Migration patterns
* Nutrient flows
* Ecological relationships

However, they are not masters of nature.

The ecosystem remains an independent force.

Native organisms are not Plastai units by default. They retain their own drives, can threaten Plastai castes and nurseries, and may resist or disrupt attempted influence.

---

# The Titan

## Central Organism

The Titan is the biological foundation of Plastai civilization.

The Titan consumes:

* Biomass
* Nutrients
* Genetic material

The Titan produces:

* Castes
* Biological infrastructure
* Adaptations
* Expanded influence capacity

The Titan fulfills a role similar to the Vanguard industrial base.

---

## Strategic Importance

The growth of the Titan represents long-term Plastai success.

Expansion is not pursued purely for territory.

Expansion is pursued to acquire:

* Biomass
* Nutrients
* Genetic diversity

The world itself is a resource.

The Titan is consciously designed toward a strategic function. Genetic assimilation can unlock unknown traits and developmental possibilities, but the Plastai commander chooses whether and how to incorporate them.

---

# Resources

## Biomass

Represents usable biological material.

Consumed by:

* Titan growth
* Caste production
* Biological construction

---

## Nutrients

Represents the energy required to sustain alien systems.

May originate from:

* Ecosystem harvesting
* Biological processing
* Specialized structures

---

## Genetic Material

Represents biological diversity.

Obtained from native species.

Used to unlock new adaptations and future caste forms.

Genetic diversity is strategically valuable.

Not all regions are equally valuable.

Genetic material is physical. It exists in organisms, samples, living repositories, or maintained developmental lines and must reach a suitable nursery process.

Learning that a trait is possible is knowledge, not a spendable genetic currency. Producing a form that uses the trait still requires compatible biological material, nursery capacity, support, and time.

---

# Castes

## Directly Controlled Units

The Plastai possess directly controlled units known as Castes.

Castes provide:

* Reconnaissance
* Expansion
* Harvesting
* Combat
* Logistics

Castes are the primary means through which players interact with the world.

---

## Adaptation

Castes are biological products of the Titan.

New forms emerge through genetic acquisition and adaptation.

Expansion into biologically diverse regions creates strategic opportunities.

---

## Biosphere Integration

Plastai Castes should appear as natural products of the world's ecology.

Their observable signatures should not make them trivially distinct from the planet's broader biosphere.

The Vanguard should struggle to distinguish:

* Wildlife
* Plastai Castes
* Plastai biological infrastructure

without direct observation.

---

# Ecological Influence

## Core Concept

Native species may offer opportunities for observation, harvest, redirection, cultivation, or integration.

Influence is not magical.

Influence requires:

* Resources
* Infrastructure
* Biological mechanisms

Influence must have a physical presence within the simulation.

Plastai ecological expertise improves selection and manipulation. It does not grant remote command, automatic allegiance, or cost-free conversion.

Influence can fail because the organism is incompatible, conditions disrupt the mechanism, upkeep lapses, the organism adapts, or an opposing intervention severs the relationship. Failed influence consumes effort and can expose Plastai presence through residue, abnormal behavior, or attempted habitat change.

---

## Broad Relevance, Limited Compatibility

Native life is broadly relevant to Plastai strategy, but relevance does not imply compatibility with influence or control.

A species may be valuable as:

* A threat
* A distraction
* A resource
* A sensor network
* A weapon

Depending on its biology, the Plastai may only be able to observe, avoid, deter, harvest, or protect it. Redirection requires a usable behavioral or ecological lever. Cultivation requires a sustainable relationship. Direct control is limited to compatible organisms with a dedicated integration mechanism.

---

## Scarcity

The Plastai cannot influence every species simultaneously.

Players must prioritize opportunities.

Plastai commanders must ask both whether a useful relationship is biologically possible and whether establishing it is worth the opportunity cost.

## Exploitation And Symbiosis

Plastai interaction with native life can include:

* Harvesting organisms for biomass or nursery inputs.
* Acquiring genetic material for adaptations and Titan development.
* Cultivating symbiotic species and trophic relationships.
* Redirecting populations through chemical or ecological cues.
* Protecting organisms that sustain alien infrastructure.
* Suppressing predators, competitors, disease, or uncontrolled species.
* Integrating exceptional organisms into direct command systems.

These relationships range from mutualism to predation. The Plastai may preserve an ecosystem because it sustains them, then overharvest another because immediate growth matters more.

Plastai activity should create ecological wakes: changed populations, missing age groups, altered migration, chemical residue, new dependencies, and pressure elsewhere in the food web. The Vanguard can observe those effects without correctly identifying harvest, cultivation, or adaptation as the cause.

See [World and Ecology](design-world-and-ecology.md) for the shared interaction model and control-state distinctions.

# Information Model

## Ecological Awareness

The Plastai possess deep understanding of biological systems.

Plastai players naturally recognize:

* Species
* Predator behavior
* Migration patterns
* Ecological disruption

The ecosystem is readable.

---

## Industrial Blindness

The Plastai do not naturally understand Vanguard systems.

Vanguard activity generates signals.

Examples:

* Heat
* Vibration
* Resource extraction
* Noise
* Construction

The Plastai must interpret those signals.

Vanguard intentions are not automatically known.

Plastai observers may detect heat, vibration, waste, repetition, and habitat change while remaining unable to distinguish production from storage, maintenance from failure, or routine traffic from strategic preparation.

Their collection advantage is local ecological context, not universal awareness. Living networks notice departures from familiar baselines, but provide poor coverage in sterile, distant, newly transformed, or biologically disconnected areas. Close investigation of industrial purpose requires time and exposure just as human ecological investigation does.

---

## Signals And Interpretation

A physical signature is a potentially detectable consequence in the world.

An observation is evidence a Plastai collector actually acquired from a signature.

A report describes that observation in Plastai ecological terms. Meaning emerges through commander interpretation.

This principle applies to both factions.

---

# Mobility

## Ecological Mobility

The Plastai project power through ecological pathways.

Examples include:

* Forest networks
* Migration routes
* Burrow systems
* River ecosystems
* Biological corridors

Plastai mobility is tied to ecological connectivity rather than infrastructure.

Every terrain may support a Plastai biological solution, including subterranean systems and subsurface aquatic environments. This does not mean every caste can traverse every terrain.

Access requires specialized organisms, adaptations, support relationships, or developmental investment. A burrower, deep-water organism, flyer, climber, and toxic-environment symbiont each solve different access problems and carry limitations outside their niche.

Plastai terrain breadth is therefore potential breadth. Committing nursery capacity and genetic design to one access form creates opportunity costs elsewhere.

---

## Species-Specific Risk

Not all native species are friendly to the Plastai.

Some species:

* Ignore the Plastai.
* Avoid the Plastai.
* Threaten the Plastai.

The ecosystem creates genuine movement constraints.

# Victory Philosophy

The Plastai are not attempting to build a larger nation-state.

Their strategic objective is to consciously design and mature a Titan-scale organism capable of permanently changing their relationship with the environment.

The mature Titan gives Plastai civilization a lasting environmental advantage and enough strategic power to clear opposing forces from the world if the Plastai player chooses. Destroying the Vanguard is not required to achieve victory; that capability is a consequence of successful maturation.

Titan development consumes physically gathered biomass, nutrients, genetic material, nursery capacity, support organisms, and time. Its chosen design determines the exact dependencies and ecological signatures.

Terminal maturation creates an unmistakable global signal and a bounded counter-operation window. If the Titan remains physically supported and survives that crisis, the Plastai win.
