# Factions

## Asymmetric Strategic Frame
- Humans pursue capability through industry, logistics, and coordinated firepower.
- Aliens pursue capability through ecosystem cultivation, adaptation, and evolutionary leverage.
- Neither side needs total map ownership to win.

## Human Faction
### Identity
- Industrial, logistical, and information-driven.
- Adapts through engineering and production.
- Converts information, resources, and logistics into force.

### Typical Human Assets
- Artillery
- Tanks
- Drones
- Sensors
- Rockets
- Depots
- Roads, rail, and bridges
- Factories
- Objective infrastructure

### Strengths
- Planning and coordination
- Range and firepower
- Ability to scale force through organized production

### Weaknesses
- Dependency on physical logistics in the world
- Fragile dependency chains: rail lines, depots, routes, bridges, convoys, power, fuel, sensors
- Cannot assume map control equals objective progress

### Notes
- Human inventory exists in the world, not magically in UI.
- Artillery can be a starting concept, but human development paths should remain flexible.

## Alien Faction
### Identity
- Ecological, adaptive, and evolutionary.
- Does not mine biomass as renamed ore.
- Cultivates living systems and harvests ecological opportunity.

### Native Ecological Context
- Wildlife, forests, fish, flyers, burrowed creatures, predators
- Migration paths and breeding grounds
- A living map that exists before faction actions

### Alien Resource Logic
- Nutrient density
- Genetic diversity
- Ecological stability
- Adaptation potential
- Biomass throughput

### Typical Alien Infrastructure Concepts
- Feeding grounds
- Nutrient reservoirs
- Adaptation chambers
- Genetic repositories
- Maturation pools
- Titan nurseries

### Strengths
- Adaptation options: burrowing, flying, swimming, camouflage, siege organisms, titan organisms
- Ability to weaponize ecological and biological processes

### Weaknesses
- Biological investment and developmental time
- Concentration risk in key ecological processes

### Notes
- Aliens can grow titan-scale organisms capable of devastating human bases if maturation is not disrupted.

## Shared Constraints
- Both factions operate on one physical map.
- Both factions act through incomplete understanding.
- Both factions can disrupt, delay, and mislead each other without total annihilation.
