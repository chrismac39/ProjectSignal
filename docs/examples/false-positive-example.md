# False Positive Example

> Status: Illustrative future example. This is not an implemented scenario contract.

## Purpose

This example demonstrates that not every observation is meaningful.

The game presents observations.

The player decides whether those observations matter.

Incorrect conclusions waste time, attention, reconnaissance assets, and operational opportunities.

---

## Omniscient Reality

A seasonal migration has begun.

Thousands of large herbivores move from a northern basin into a central valley.

No alien activity exists in the area.

No human activity exists in the area.

The event is entirely natural.

---

## Vanguard Reality

The commander observes:

* Large wildlife movement.
* Increased thermal activity from animal concentrations.
* Significant ecological change in a previously quiet region.

The game provides no explanation.

The commander must decide what it means.

Possible interpretations include:

* Seasonal migration.
* Alien feeding activity.
* Adaptation site development.
* Something else entirely.

The game does not identify the correct answer.

---

## Plastai Reality

The migration is expected.

The ecosystem is behaving normally.

The alien player understands the movement immediately.

No action is taken.

---

## Turn Sequence

### Turn 22

Wildlife begins moving south.

The map changes.

The player notices.

Or does not notice.

---

### Turn 23

The migration expands.

The area appears increasingly active.

No notifications explain why.

---

### Turn 24

The human commander faces a choice:

* Ignore it.
* Monitor it.
* Send scouts.
* Divert reconnaissance assets.
* Launch a strike on destination or source valley anticipating alien activity involved.

The game offers no recommendation.

---

### Turn 25

A drone reaches the area.

Observation:

Wildlife.

Nothing else.

---

### Turn 26

The commander now understands what was happening.

The observation has been explained through investigation.

Not through the interface.

---

## Alternative Outcome

The commander chooses not to investigate.

Nothing happens.

The migration continues.

The signal disappears naturally.

The player never learns whether it mattered.

---

Another commander launches artillery.

The artillery destroys wildlife.

No enemy capability is degraded.

Ammunition is wasted.

---

## Design Lesson

The observation was real.

The conclusion was wrong.

This distinction is critical.

The game should not create fake information.

The game should create real information that can be misunderstood.

---

## Replay View

Post-match replay reveals:

The valley contained only a seasonal migration.

No alien activity existed.

The replay does not tell the player:

"You should not have investigated."

Instead it reveals reality and allows the player to evaluate their own decision making.

The lesson comes from comparison between perception and reality, not from automated coaching.
