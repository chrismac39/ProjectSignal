# Prototype Scenarios

## Purpose

Prototype Scenarios exist to validate the core design assumptions of ProjectSignal.

These are not gameplay missions.

They are design tests.

Each scenario should answer a specific question about the information model, perception layers, reconnaissance systems, deception mechanics, or replay functionality.

The purpose of Prototype 0 is not to prove combat is fun.

The purpose of Prototype 0 is to prove that Human Reality, Alien Reality, and Omniscient Reality create interesting decisions.

---

## Scenario Format

Each scenario should define:

* What exists in Omniscient Reality.
* What Human Reality shows.
* What Alien Reality shows.
* What decision the player faces.
* What success looks like.
* What failure looks like.
* What the replay should reveal.

Scenarios should focus on information and decision-making rather than combat effectiveness.

---

# Scenario 1: Wildlife Migration

## Omniscient Reality

A large seasonal migration is occurring.

No human activity is involved.

No alien activity is involved.

The event is entirely natural.

---

## Human Reality

The commander observes:

* Large wildlife movement.
* Significant ecosystem activity.
* New concentrations of animals.

No explanation is provided.

---

## Alien Reality

The ecosystem is behaving normally.

The migration is expected.

No action is required.

---

## Player Choice

The human commander may:

* Ignore the event.
* Monitor the event.
* Send scouts.
* Redirect reconnaissance.
* Launch a strike on the source or destination region.

---

## Success

The player forms hypotheses and makes a deliberate decision.

The player treats the signal as uncertain.

---

## Failure

The player immediately knows the answer.

The signal becomes a solved problem rather than an intelligence problem.

---

## Replay Reveals

The migration was natural.

The observation was real.

The player's interpretation may or may not have been correct.

---

# Scenario 2: Industrial Disturbance

## Omniscient Reality

A human logistics route supplies a hidden ammunition depot.

The depot supports several military assets.

---

## Alien Reality

The alien player observes:

* Ecological disruption.
* Wildlife avoidance.
* Repeated movement patterns.

No explanation is provided.

---

## Human Reality

The route appears routine.

No threat is perceived.

---

## Player Choice

The alien commander may:

* Ignore the disturbance.
* Continue observation.
* Send scouts.
* Attack the route.
* Search for the destination.

---

## Success

The player attempts to infer dependency from patterns.

---

## Failure

The player only reacts to obvious military assets.

---

## Replay Reveals

The route mattered more than the visible combat units it supplied.

---

# Scenario 3: False Positive

## Omniscient Reality

A seasonal migration creates unusual ecosystem activity.

No enemy activity exists.

---

## Human Reality

The commander observes:

* Wildlife movement.
* Ecological disruption.
* Thermal activity from animal concentrations.

No explanation is provided.

---

## Alien Reality

The event is normal ecosystem behavior.

---

## Player Choice

The commander may:

* Ignore the signal.
* Monitor it.
* Investigate it.
* Strike the source region.
* Strike the destination region.

---

## Success

The player understands that observations are not conclusions.

---

## Failure

Every unusual event is assumed to be enemy activity.

---

## Replay Reveals

The observation was genuine.

The interpretation may have been incorrect.

# Scenario 4: Decoy Signal

## Omniscient Reality

A human commander operates:

* One real logistics facility.
* One fake industrial site.

The decoy generates believable signals but possesses little strategic value.

---

## Alien Reality

Both locations appear suspicious.

Neither location is identified as a decoy.

---

## Human Reality

The commander knows which site is real.

The commander does not know whether the deception is succeeding.

---

## Player Choice

The alien commander must decide:

* Which location deserves investigation.
* Whether to attack before confirmation.
* Whether to ignore either signal.

---

## Success

The decoy influences enemy attention and decision-making.

---

## Failure

The decoy is either useless or perfectly convincing.

---

## Replay Reveals

Whether the decoy consumed attention, resources, or reconnaissance effort.

---

# Scenario 5: Hidden Logistics

## Omniscient Reality

Human artillery depends on a concealed ammunition route and depot.

The artillery itself is highly visible.

The dependency is not.

---

## Human Reality

The commander focuses on the artillery batteries.

The supply network appears routine.

---

## Alien Reality

The alien player observes:

* Ecological disruption.
* Convoy patterns.
* Repeated movement.

The underlying purpose remains unknown.

---

## Player Choice

The alien commander may:

* Attack the artillery.
* Investigate the route.
* Search for dependencies.
* Ignore the activity.

---

## Success

The player identifies that dependencies may be more important than frontline assets.

---

## Failure

The player only targets visible military power.

---

## Replay Reveals

The destruction of the supply network would have been more damaging than destruction of the artillery itself.

---

# Scenario 6: Titan Warning Signs

## Omniscient Reality

A Titan Organism is under development.

The process affects surrounding ecosystems long before the Titan appears.

---

## Human Reality

The commander observes:

* Migration changes.
* Population shifts.
* Ecological disruption.
* Unusual biological activity.

No direct evidence of a Titan exists.

---

## Alien Reality

The alien player understands that Titan development is underway.

Protecting the process becomes important.

---

## Player Choice

The human commander may:

* Ignore the signals.
* Monitor them.
* Scout the region.
* Conduct area bombardment.
* Seek precision targeting information.

---

## Success

The player recognizes that strategic targets require understanding and reconnaissance.

---

## Failure

The player expects random bombardment to reliably destroy major strategic assets.

---

## Replay Reveals

The warning signs existed long before the Titan emerged.

The challenge was interpreting them correctly.

---

# Scenario 7: Three Reality Replay

## Omniscient Reality

Both factions act on incomplete information.

Both commanders possess incorrect assumptions.

---

## Human Reality

The commander believes a particular explanation for battlefield events.

---

## Alien Reality

The alien commander believes a different explanation.

---

## Player Choice

No decision is made during replay.

This scenario validates replay functionality.

---

## Success

The replay explains:

* What each commander knew.
* What each commander believed.
* What was actually true.

---

## Failure

The replay becomes a simple event log.

The perception gap is lost.

---

## Replay Reveals

The difference between:

* Human Reality.
* Alien Reality.
* Omniscient Reality.

This difference is the central design concept of ProjectSignal.

---

# Prototype Success Criteria

Prototype 0 is successful if players:

* Form hypotheses.
* Make decisions under uncertainty.
* Investigate observations.
* Misinterpret information occasionally.
* Learn through replay analysis.

Prototype 0 is unsuccessful if players:

* Immediately know what observations mean.
* Treat reconnaissance as a formality.
* Receive automated strategic conclusions.
* Ignore the replay because it adds no new understanding.

The prototype exists to validate the information game before combat, production, logistics, adaptation, or victory systems are expanded.
