# Replay Example

> Status: Illustrative future example. The current replay interface is a Markdown AAR and JSON Lines records.

## Purpose

This example demonstrates the role of the Omniscient Replay.

The replay is not merely a record of battlefield events.

It is a comparison between:

* Vanguard Reality
* Plastai Reality
* Omniscient Reality

The replay reveals where assumptions were correct, where they were incorrect, and how those assumptions influenced decisions.

The replay should teach players without providing assistance during gameplay.

---

## Match Summary

Vanguard Objective:

Construct and launch an evacuation rocket.

Plastai Objective:

Complete a Titan Organism and establish a mature ecosystem network.

Neither side is attempting direct annihilation.

Both sides interfere with each other because doing so improves their own chances of success.

---

## Vanguard Understanding During The Match

The Vanguard commander believes:

* The eastern refinery decoy is attracting Plastai attention.
* The northern valley is likely the center of Plastai activity.
* The western logistics corridor is secure.
* The Titan threat has been delayed significantly.

Based on these assumptions:

* Additional resources are invested into the eastern decoy.
* Artillery focuses on the northern valley.
* Reconnaissance is redirected away from the west.
* Rocket construction continues.

---

## Plastai Understanding During The Match

The Plastai commander believes:

* The eastern refinery is a decoy.
* The western logistics corridor supports critical Vanguard infrastructure.
* Vanguard attention is focused on the wrong region.
* Titan development remains concealed.

Based on these assumptions:

* Scouts are withdrawn from the eastern basin.
* Reconnaissance concentrates on western supply routes.
* Raids target logistics rather than artillery.
* Titan development continues.

---

## Omniscient Reality

The replay reveals:

* The eastern refinery decoy was exposed several turns earlier.
* The Vanguard commander never learned this.
* The northern valley contained only minor Plastai activity.
* The western logistics corridor supplied rocket construction.
* Plastai scouts correctly identified the dependency.
* Vanguard artillery repeatedly struck low-value targets.

Neither commander possessed a complete picture.

## What The Replay Reveals

The replay does not say:

"You should have done X."

The replay does not provide a score for decision quality.

Instead it reveals:

* What each commander knew.
* What each commander believed.
* What was actually true.

Players draw their own conclusions.

---

## Example Replay Questions

The replay encourages questions such as:

* Why did I believe the decoy was still working?
* Why did I ignore the western corridor?
* Why did the Plastai player prioritize that region?
* When did the Titan project become detectable?
* What clues did I overlook?
* Which assumptions were correct?

The goal is understanding.

Not coaching.

---

## Design Lesson

Many strategy games create drama through hidden information.

ProjectSignal creates drama through misunderstood information.

The replay is where misunderstanding becomes visible.

Players discover not only what happened.

They discover why they believed something else was happening.

---

## Core Principle

The replay is the only place where objective reality exists.

During the match, every commander operates within an incomplete and imperfect understanding of the battlefield.

The replay reveals the gap between perception and reality.

That gap is the heart of the game.
